# The World in One Day — Wednesday, 18 November 2026

One 1024x1024 news poster summing up Wednesday 18 November 2026: Iran's strikes on Kuwait and Bahrain after the U.S. hit on Qeshm Island, the Kerch Bridge arch collapse, Xi and Putin meeting in Shenzhen ahead of the Trump trilateral, Democrats taking the U.S. Senate 56-44, the U.S. losing measles-free status, Ebola passing 11,000 cases in Congo, Voyager 1 reaching one light-day, and Nvidia's record quarter. Every headline is drawn from reporting by Reuters, AP, the BBC, NASA and PAHO on the day; the poster itself is drawn from scratch with PIL (`render_poster.py`), using flat vector-style icons and no photos.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This copies `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `/output`).
